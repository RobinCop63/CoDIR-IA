## Finding

- The package and the documentation designate the publisher as **Vauquigneux Gestion SASU** (SIREN **102 885 654**, 6 Cours des Vauquigneux, 35310 Mordelles, France).
- The public register (Annuaire des Entreprises / RNE) shows the generic form **SAS**.

## Analysis

A SASU is a simplified joint-stock company with a single shareholder: the register’s “SAS” and the documentation’s “SASU” are not contradictory. The national register records the generic legal form; the single-shareholder character is a factual state of the shareholding, which may change without any change of form.

## Rule for contracts

Any final contract (DPA, B2B contract, signed licence, agreement) must reproduce **exactly** the name and legal form shown on the RNE/Kbis extract retained on the day of signature, with the SIREN. If a template in the documentation differs from the extract, **the extract prevails** and the template is corrected before signature.

## Scope

This note is a contract-drafting rule. It changes neither the product licence, nor the ownership of rights, nor the AI Act marking (publisher identity governed by `application/version.py`, constant `PUBLISHER`). Rights in the CoDIR IA trademark remain separate from software licensing; see `TRADEMARKS.md`. An address in documentation is not evidence of operational receipt.
